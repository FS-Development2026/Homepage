Why I Started FS-Development

Hey, I'm Paul! After working on several FiveM projects and seeing how hard it is for new or passionate server owners to afford high-quality, modern scripts, I decided to launch FS-Development on my own.

Right now, we are right at the beginning of this journey. I design, code, test, and maintain everything completely solo — using AI as a smart co-pilot to build futuristic ideas faster, cleaner, and completely open source so anyone can learn from and customize the code.
